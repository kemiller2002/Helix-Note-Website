FAQ

A few practical questions people ask early.

Is HelixNote only for medical cases?

No. The core value is structured, evidence-linked documentation across time. Medical is the clearest use case, but the same structure applies to research, compliance, and investigations.

What does “AMA-style citations” mean here?

Reports can include numbered references and a bibliography-style section so claims are traceable back to source notes, documents, or external references.

Can I export something I can hand to a specialist?

Yes—HelixNote is designed to produce clean, printable briefs and appendices (PDF/HTML), with an emphasis on readability and tight scope.
